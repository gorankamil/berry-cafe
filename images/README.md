# Put your photos here 📸

Upload all of Berry Cafe's images into this folder.

## Helpful to include

- Your **logo**, as a PNG with a see-through background if you have one
- A big **welcome photo** for the top of the page, like the storefront or the inside of the cafe
- **Food and drink** photos
- **Interior and atmosphere** shots
- Photos of **you or your team**, if you want them on the site

## Tips

- **File types:** `.jpg`, `.png` or `.webp` all work.
- **File names:** simple lowercase names with dashes are easiest,
  like `storefront.jpg` or `berry-latte.jpg`. Don't worry if yours aren't; Claude can rename them.
- **Size:** each file must be under 25 MB to upload on GitHub. Phone photos are fine.
