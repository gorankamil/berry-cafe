Our Story

Every corner tells a different story

Since 2022, Berry Cafe has been the place where passion meets coffee.

Step inside and you'll find dusty teal walls lined with vintage frames, old clocks and candlesticks, and warm wooden tables. It's a place to linger over a latte, catch up with friends, or simply slow down for a while.

Behind the counter, the Berry Cafe family makes everything from a classic Ottoman coffee to our signature Berry Special mixes, served alongside fresh cookies, croissants and cakes.
